# Glossary

- Yolculuk: PST coaches' pre-built, multi-part program composed of modules, workshops, and e-books.
- Modul: Thematic content group composed of ordered packages.
- Paket: A module sub-unit with focused readings and exercises; may be locked by prerequisite completion.
- Atolye: Interactive content with readings and exercises plus guided reflections.
- e-Kitap: Digital book in the PST library with chapters and reading progress tracking.
- Abonelik: Subscription plan granting library access; status can be active, trial, or canceled with grace.
- Plan Sahibi: Subscription owner who can manage plan, add-ons, and seats.
- Uye: A subscription member with access but no purchase rights.
- Add-on: Optional entitlement add-ons (AI Package, Coaching School, Extra Seats).
- Icerik Asistani: Guided flow that recommends content based on goals and preferences.
- Ilerleme: User's completion state and metrics across content types.
- Gunluk Hedef: Daily target set when starting content; used for progress pacing.
- Teslim: Submitting a reflection/comment before the daily deadline.
- Kilitli Ilerleme: Rule that prevents access to next step until current is completed.
- Favoriler: Saved highlights/notes/content for later access and export.
- Koleksiyon: User-defined grouping of favorites.
- Gelisim Raporu: Exportable progress summary (PDF) with privacy consent.
